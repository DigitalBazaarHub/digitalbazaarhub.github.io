🤝 Community Rules

  • 1
    Be kind and respectful to all members. We're here to support and uplift each other in our professional journeys.
  • 2
    Share knowledge and opportunities freely. The bazaar thrives on open exchange of ideas and connections.
  • 3
    Keep discussions constructive and relevant to tech, business, and the digital ecosystem.
  • 4
    No spam, no hard selling. Share your work naturally and respect the community's attention.
  • 5
    Maintain professionalism while keeping the warm, informal spirit that makes our community special.
  • 6
    Moderators reserve the right to remove disruptive members at any time, no warnings required.
  • 7
    Don't be an asshole. Zero tolerance for racism, sexism, harassment, or toxic behavior of any kind.
