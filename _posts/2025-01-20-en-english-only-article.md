## Why Single-Language Articles?

Sometimes content is tailored for a specific audience, or the writer is more comfortable expressing ideas in one language.

<div class="pull-quote">
Not every piece of content needs translation — what matters is reaching the right audience.
</div>

## Benefits of Focused Content

- **Authenticity**: Writing in your native language feels more natural and genuine
- **Speed**: No waiting for translations
- **Relevance**: Some topics are specific to certain communities
