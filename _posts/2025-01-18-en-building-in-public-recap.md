## Session Overview

On January 18th, we hosted a fireside chat with three founders who've embraced building in public. The session was moderated by Ahmad and Sara, and featured candid conversations about transparency, failure, and trust-building.

## Key Takeaways

### 1. Vulnerability builds trust

All three founders agreed: sharing failures openly attracted more support than only celebrating wins. One founder noted that their most engaged followers came after a post about a failed product launch.

### 2. Choose your level of transparency

Building in public doesn't mean sharing everything. The panel discussed finding the right balance:

- **Revenue numbers** — Some share, some don't
- **Technical challenges** — Generally safe and valuable to share
- **Team issues** — Usually best kept private
- **Customer feedback** — Anonymized learnings work well

### 3. Consistency matters more than frequency

Weekly updates beat sporadic viral posts. The audience learns to expect and look forward to your rhythm.
